Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries every fixture's wastewater out of a Dania Beach house toward the street connection. When that one pipe is blocked, the lowest drains in the home fill first.

Call (954) 697-4920

Main Sewer Line Blocked? Stop the Water and Avoid a Sewage Backup

The line out of your house is blocked if the toilets, tubs and floor drains all struggle together, and that is an emergency we answer 24/7. Stop every source of water right away, including the washer and dishwasher. Do not flush to test whether it has cleared.

The lowest drain, often a tub or shower on the ground floor, will overflow first. Pull towels and rugs back from it and keep electronics off the floor. If sewage is touching an outlet or appliance, leave that area alone and tell us when we answer.

Is It the Main Line or Just One Drain?

A single clogged fixture is a drain cleaning job. A blocked main line shows up in a pattern: more than one fixture slow at once, bubbling in a toilet when the washer drains, or water backing into the lowest tub. Sewage smell in the yard, or a wet patch that does not dry, points the same way.

If only the kitchen sink is slow, start with drain cleaning. If the whole house shares the trouble, the main line is the place to look, and the cleanout is the way in.

How a Sewer Line Cleaning Visit Runs

The sequence stays the same on every call, so nothing is skipped.

  1. We locate the cleanout, a capped fitting of about 4 inches that gives direct access to the line, and check the cap and surrounding soil.
  2. We open it slowly, because standing sewage may be under pressure behind the cap, and let the line settle.
  3. We feed a sewer machine cable to the blockage and cut through it, noting the distance where the cable met resistance.
  4. We pull back and run clean water to confirm flow, or move to a jetter if the wall is coated.
  5. We record the footage or distance, so you know where the trouble sat and can tell us if it returns.

What to Check First at the Cleanout

You can do two or three things before we arrive, all from outside the house.

  • Find the cleanout cap and confirm it is seated. A loose cap lets yard water and silt into the line.
  • Clear mulch, leaves and toys off it, and mark it so the crew can find it in the dark.
  • Do not open it yourself. If the line is full, the water that comes out is raw sewage.
  • If the cap is buried and you plan to dig it out, call Sunshine 811 first. Florida requires notice at least two full business days before digging, and weekends and holidays do not count.
  • Tell us if a tree or palm grows near the lateral.

Septic Tank or City Sewer? Check Before You Call

Most of Dania Beach drains to a city sewer, but we found no public source that lists which streets are on septic, so we will not guess. Your water and sewer bill shows whether the city charges you for sewer service. If it does not, look for a round or square lid in the yard and tell us about it when you call.

A full septic tank needs a pump truck and a snake will not help. We will say so on the call. If the system is septic, slow drains in every fixture, a wet spot over the drain field and sewage smells outside are the signs to report.

What Dania Beach Lines Face: Roots, Silt and Storm Flow

Census data lists 1977 as the median year a Dania Beach home was built, so a typical lateral has decades of service behind it. Angi's pipe guide calls clay sewer pipe vulnerable to root intrusion and says cast iron can corrode and clog. We cannot say which material your house has, and no local survey tells us how common either is, so we read the cable and the camera instead of assuming.

Flooding matters too. The April 12 to 13, 2023 floods and tornado led Dania Beach to declare a state of emergency, and a yard that stood in water can send silt into a cleanout with a loose cap. After a flood, check the cap, and if the line drains slowly afterward, Call (954) 697-4920 before the next storm. Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so a cleaning before June beats a backup in August.

After the Line Is Open: Keeping It That Way

Cleaning removes the blockage but not the reason for it. We tell you what came out and what to change: grease down the kitchen sink, wipes sold as flushable, or a tree whose roots found the joint. If the line clogs more than once a year, a sewer camera inspection is the next step.

Wastewater from Dania Beach flows on to Hollywood's Southern Regional plant. A clear lateral on your side does not help if the street main is slow, so ask your city sewer office whether neighbors report the same trouble.

Some homes also have a sump or an ejector pump that lifts waste or ground water up to the sewer. A pump that hums but does not move water, or a basin that fills and stays full, is a different problem from a clog in the line. Describe what you hear and we will tell you what to check.

Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Ranges

National published guides put main line cleaning at about $200 to $600, averaging roughly $250 (Fixr), and Florida prices vary. Root removal is listed separately by Angi at $100 to $600.

What moves the price

  • Line length. The farther the blockage from the cleanout, the more cable and time it takes.
  • Blockage type. Paper is quick. Roots and compacted grease take more work.
  • Cleanout access. A missing or buried cleanout adds digging or a fixture removal.
  • Follow-up method. Jetting or a camera pass adds equipment and time.
  • Hour of the call. Ask on the call whether nights, weekends or holidays are priced differently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle sewer line cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Dania?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, including holidays, and a blocked main line gets emergency priority. We cannot promise an arrival time, but we will tell you what to do with your water use until we get there.

How do I find my sewer cleanout?

Look for a round capped pipe about 4 inches wide near the house wall, the property edge, or the side yard. If you cannot find one, describe the layout and we will help on the phone.

Is the blockage my responsibility or the city's?

We cannot tell by phone who owns which stretch of line. Ask the Dania Beach sewer office. If water stands at your cleanout, the blockage is farther down the line, and our camera can show where it sits.

How often should a main line be cleaned?

There is no fixed interval. A line with no history of trouble may need nothing, while one with roots or grease may need attention yearly. We tell you what we found and what to watch.

Can I run a cable myself?

A rented machine can injure you and can jam in a damaged pipe. A cable that sticks in a cracked pipe can make the damage worse. We would rather you call first.

Do you also take calls outside Dania?

Yes, we dispatch into Pembroke Park and West Park from the same line. Call and give your address.

Code reference: plumbing work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (8th Edition, 2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether your job needs a permit.
