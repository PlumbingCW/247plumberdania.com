What to Tell Us When You Call

A short description saves minutes. The dispatcher may ask the following.

  • Your street address and nearest cross street
  • Where the water is coming from and whether it is still running
  • Whether the main valve is closed
  • Whether any power outlets or the water heater are near the water
  • Whether you own the property, rent it or manage it

Gas Smell or Danger to Life

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 first, then the gas utility. For flooding near live electrical equipment, stay out of the water and call 911 if anyone is at risk. Plumbing calls come after the danger is handled.
