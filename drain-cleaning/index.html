Emergency Drain Cleaning in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Drain cleaning clears whatever stops a sink, tub, shower or floor drain from emptying, and it is the call we hear most often in Dania, FL. We work at the fixture, along the branch line, and out to the 4-inch cleanout in the yard.

Drain Backing Up Right Now? Do This First

A drain that backs up at night will not wait for business hours, and our dispatch line runs 24/7 for exactly that call. While you wait, stop running water anywhere in the house. Every flush and every load of laundry pushes more water toward the blockage.

If dirty water is rising in a tub or floor drain, keep children and pets out of the room. Stay clear of wet floors near outlets, power strips and appliances. Tell us on the phone if sewage has reached carpet or a baseboard, and we will talk through what is safe to touch.

Why Do All My Drains Gurgle When I Flush One Toilet?

Gurgling in one fixture while another drains means air is fighting its way past a blockage. A single slow bathroom sink usually points to a clog in its own P-trap or a short branch line. When the tub, the sink and the toilet misbehave together, the blockage sits farther down, in a line they all share.

That difference shapes the whole job. A fixture clog is often a short visit with a hand auger or a drum machine. A main line clog needs a longer cable fed through the cleanout, and sometimes a camera to see what the cable hit.

Kitchen Sink Clogs: Grease, Food Scraps and the Disposal

Kitchen sinks clog differently from bathroom sinks. Cooking grease runs down the drain as a liquid, cools on the pipe wall and builds into a coating that catches food scraps. The sink drains slower each week until one day it stops. Hot water and soap can push the grease further along, and it settles in a worse spot.

If the sink has a disposal and water stands on both bowls, the blockage is often past the trap. Skip the plunger if you have poured drain cleaner, because splashing it is a safety risk. Turn off the disposal, bail out the water and call. We pull the trap first, then use a cable on the line behind it.

Cable, Auger or Jetter: How We Choose

A hand auger reaches a few feet past a sink trap. A drum machine feeds a longer cable through a tub drain or a cleanout and chews through hair, soap and paper. Trade guides describe the limit of a cable plainly: it punches a hole through a clog but does not scrub the pipe wall.

When grease or scale coats the wall, the blockage returns in weeks, and we say so before you pay for a second cabling. Hydro jetting scours the full pipe circumference and is the better follow-up in that case. If the cable stops at the same spot twice, we recommend a sewer camera inspection because a collapsed or offset pipe will not clear no matter how hard we cut.

What to Do Before We Arrive

These steps keep the damage small and give the technician a clear start. None of them needs tools.

  • Stop using every sink, tub, shower, toilet and the washing machine.
  • Find the cleanout, usually a capped fitting about 4 inches wide near the house wall or in the yard, and clear leaves and mulch off the cap. Do not open it, because standing sewage can rush out.
  • Lift rugs, boxes and electronics away from low drains and the lowest bathroom.
  • Skip chemical drain openers. They can splash back on skin and leave caustic water sitting in the pipe where we have to work.
  • Write down which fixtures are affected, when it started, and whether a neighbor has the same trouble.

Why the Same Drain Clogs Again Every Few Weeks

A repeat clog has a cause the first cleaning did not remove. Kitchen lines collect cooled grease, bathroom lines collect hair bound with soap, and older lines collect scale and roots. Cleaning only the middle of the clog leaves the coating that catches the next one.

We note where the cable met resistance and what came back on the head. That tells us whether to recommend jetting, a full sewer line cleaning, or a camera first. Call (954) 697-4920 if a drain you paid to clear has already slowed again.

A drain line can also leak instead of clog. A stain on the ceiling below a bathroom, a damp cleanout or a smell near a wall can mean the pipe itself is cracked or a joint has separated. A cable does not fix that. We look for it with a camera when a drain is clear but water still shows up where it should not.

The ACS counts 2,554 Dania Beach housing units in buildings of 20 to 49 units and 1,860 in buildings of 50 or more. In those buildings one shared stack can back up several apartments. Tell us which units are affected and which floor is lowest, because the lowest unit is where the water shows first.

Give us the cleanout location if you know it and a contact who can open the unit. We will also tell you whether the blockage looks like tenant misuse or a failing line, which matters for who pays. We also take calls from Davie and Pembroke Park properties.

What Moves the Price of a Drain Cleaning

National published guides put basic drain snaking at about $147 to $352 (HomeAdvisor) and $125 to $360 (Forbes), and Florida prices vary. Main line work and jetting cost more because the equipment and time are larger.

What moves the price

  • Access. A fixture with a removable trap or a clear cleanout is quicker than a line that needs a toilet pulled to reach it.
  • Length and depth. A clog three feet from the sink is a different job than one sixty feet down the main line.
  • Cause. Hair is easy. Grease, scale and roots may call for jetting after the cable.
  • Camera needed. If the cable keeps catching, a camera run adds time but prevents a repeat visit.
  • Hour of the call. Ask on the call whether nights, weekends or holidays are priced differently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Dania?

Yes. Our dispatch line runs 24/7, including weekends and holidays, and sewage backing into living space is treated as an emergency. We cannot promise an arrival time because it depends on where the crew is, but we will tell you what to do while you wait.

Is it safe to pour drain cleaner when a drain backs up?

We advise against it. Chemical openers can splash on skin and eyes, and if they fail they leave caustic water in the pipe for the plumber to work around. Tell us what you already poured so we can wear the right gear.

How do I know if the clog is in my pipe or the city sewer?

If the cleanout shows standing water, the blockage is farther along the line, and that stretch could be yours or the city's. We cannot tell by phone who owns which section. Ask the Dania Beach sewer office, and we can run a camera to show exactly where the problem sits.

Will a drain cable damage my old pipes?

A cable can catch on a cracked or offset section of clay or cast iron. That is why we stop and run a camera when the cable hits the same snag twice, rather than forcing it.

I manage several units. Can one visit cover a shared stack?

Yes, if you can give us access to the lowest affected unit and the cleanout. We clear the shared line first, then check each unit's fixtures so the same backup does not return next week.

Why does my tub gurgle when the washing machine drains?

The washer dumps water fast enough to push air through a partly blocked line, and the tub is the nearest open vent path. It is an early warning of a clog in the shared branch. Call (954) 697-4920 before it turns into a full backup.

Do you clear a clogged yard or ground-level drain?

We can look at a clogged yard or ground-level drain. Tell us whether it takes roof or yard water, since that line can follow a different path from the house drain, and how long the water stands after rain.

Code reference: plumbing work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (8th Edition, 2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether your job needs a permit.
