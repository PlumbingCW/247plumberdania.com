Water Heater Installation in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Water heater installation in Dania Beach is the planned version of a swap: you choose the fuel, size and location before the old tank fails. In Dania, FL the job comes with a permit, a drain pan where required, a relief valve discharge pipe and, for gas units, a vent and a gas shutoff.

The permit comes first

Water heater replacement needs a permit in Broward County jurisdictions, and a new installation follows the same path. Broward Board Policy 22-02 sets out a water heater data form that goes with the county's Uniform Building Permit Application.

The form asks for the old and new unit type, gallons, wire and breaker size or gas input and flue size, plus the manufacturer's installation instructions and efficiency proof. The City of Dania Beach Building Division runs permits through its own online portal, so ask there which documents the city wants for your address.

Check any plumber against the Florida license lookup at myfloridalicense.com. A permit pulled by an unlicensed person is a red flag, not a shortcut.

  • Make, model, tank gallons and fuel input from the data plate.
  • Wire size and breaker size for electric, or gas pipe size and flue size for gas.
  • Manufacturer installation instructions.
  • Efficiency proof, such as an AHRI certificate or manufacturer data.

Parts that belong in a careful installation

A water heater is not just a tank on a floor. Several small parts protect the home when something goes wrong, and an inspector looks for them.

  • Expansion tank: when a backflow device or pressure reducing valve closes the system, heated water has nowhere to go. A thermal expansion tank on the cold line absorbs it. Plumbing guidance for Florida calls for a way to control that pressure.
  • Drain pan with a drain line: Florida cities commonly require a pan when the heater sits in living space or an attic. Ask the Building Division about your case.
  • T and P relief valve discharge pipe: runs to the floor, a pan or outdoors, never capped, and never so small it restricts flow.
  • Full-open shutoff valve on the cold inlet, so the next repair does not mean shutting off the house.
  • Flexible stainless connectors and dielectric nipples or unions between copper and steel.
  • For gas units, a sediment trap (drip leg) and an accessible gas shutoff ahead of the control.

Gas line, venting, and where the gas comes from

Peoples Gas lists Dania and Dania Beach among the Broward communities it serves. Natural gas is only available where a main runs on your street, so check your last gas bill or look for a meter before you plan a gas unit.

Gas units also need a vent. An atmospheric draft vent rises through the roof, while a power vent or direct vent unit can exhaust through a sidewall with PVC. Each type has its own clearance and combustion air rules, and the Broward form asks for the flue size.

If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call 911, then call the gas utility. Do not use a light switch or a phone inside.

Closet, garage, attic, condo, or mobile home

The median home in Dania Beach dates from 1977, per Census survey data, so many closets were built for a smaller, older heater. A taller or wider tank may need the door framing or a platform changed.

Only about 22 percent of homes are detached single-family houses. In a condo, check your association rules before you choose gas or electric, and plan for a pan and a drain that will not flood the unit below.

Roughly 12 percent of Dania Beach housing is mobile homes. Those utility closets are small, so give us the make and model of the old unit before you shop. If you are comparing a tank with a tankless water heater, space is often the deciding factor.

What to check first before you schedule

Gather these before the visit so the price and the permit are accurate.

  • Fuel type today and the fuel you want.
  • Tank size on the data plate, plus the number of people in the home.
  • Photos of the closet, venting and the top of the old heater.
  • Whether your street has a gas main, and where the meter is.
  • Condo or HOA rules about work hours and water shutoff.
  • Any recent water leaks near the heater, which can mean a bigger repair.

How installation day runs

A typical day follows a fixed order so nothing is energized before it is full and tested.

  1. Shut off water and fuel, drain the old tank, and carry it out.
  2. Set a pan if needed, place the new tank, and make the water connections.
  3. Install the expansion tank if the system calls for it, then run the relief valve pipe to its termination point.
  4. Connect the vent and gas line, or the electric wiring and breaker.
  5. Purge air from the tank through a hot tap, then light the burner or restore power.
  6. Leak test every joint, check for gas odor, check draft, and go over the controls with you.

What water heater installation costs

National published guides put an installed tank water heater at about $600 to $2,500, and an installed tankless unit at about $1,000 to $3,900. Florida prices vary.

The final price depends on fuel, tank size, and how much existing pipe, vent or wiring needs to change.

What moves the price

  • Fuel and size. A 50-gallon gas tank and a 40-gallon electric tank cost different amounts and use different parts.
  • Venting changes. A new flue, a sidewall vent or a different vent type adds parts and labor.
  • Gas line work. If the old line is too small or too far, pipe is added or replaced.
  • Code parts. The pan, the discharge pipe, the expansion tank and the permit are part of the quote.
  • Location. Attics, upper floors and tight closets cost more labor than a garage floor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to install a water heater in Dania Beach?

In Broward County jurisdictions, water heater replacement needs a permit, and so does a new install. Ask the Dania Beach Building Division what it requires for your address, since the city runs its own process.

Do I need an expansion tank?

If your plumbing has a backflow preventer or pressure reducing valve that closes the system, you need a way to control pressure when the water heats. An expansion tank is the common answer. We check your setup and explain it before we price it.

Where can the relief valve pipe end?

It must end where hot water cannot hurt anyone, such as a floor drain, an outdoor spot, or a pan with a drain line. It should not be capped or reduced in size. Your inspector may have local preferences.

Is natural gas available at my address in Dania Beach?

Peoples Gas serves parts of Broward including Dania Beach, but service depends on the street. Check for a gas meter or a recent bill, or ask the gas utility. We can also size the pipe once we know the supply.

Can you install in a condo or an apartment?

Yes, with the building's rules in mind. Tell us whether your association needs notice, which hours work is allowed, and where the building's shutoff is. Call (954) 697-4920 to plan the visit around those rules.

How big a tank do I need?

Size is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow EnergyGuide label, not just the gallons. A bigger tank loses more heat while it sits. We work from your household and your fixtures, not a rule of thumb.

I manage buildings near Dania Beach. Do you cover installs in nearby towns?

Yes. We serve West Park and nearby areas from Dania Beach. Send the list of units and fuel types, and we plan them in order.

Code reference: plumbing work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (8th Edition, 2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether your job needs a permit.
