Dania Beach: Our Home Base

Dania Beach is ZIP 33004 and the place our crews leave from. The city runs its own water system, so a pressure or meter question starts with city utility customer service and not with a county office. Wastewater flows to the City of Hollywood's Southern Regional plant.

The median home was built in 1977, and about 12.4 percent of units are mobile homes. Gas, where it is piped, comes from Peoples Gas. If you smell it, leave first and call 911 before you call a plumber. Storms matter here too, because the April 12 and 13, 2023 tornado and flooding put water into a lot of low yards.

Davie: Big Town, Several Water Utilities

Davie had 105,691 residents in the 2020 Census, and its lots range from townhomes to horse properties under equestrian zoning. The Town of Davie serves only part of the town with water. Sunrise, Femcrest and Broward County serve other areas.

That changes the first call. Your water bill tells you who owns the meter and the main, and it tells us where our responsibility starts. In June 2017 about 11 to 12 inches of rain fell in 24 hours, so canal-side streets get a flood checklist on our Davie page.

Pembroke Park: Mobile Homes and Rentals

Pembroke Park is small, with about 6,260 residents. Of its 3,698 housing units, about 53 percent are mobile homes, and the town counts 17 mobile home parks. About 68 percent of households rent, so many calls involve a landlord or park manager as well as the occupant.

The town has its own building department and its own sewer collection. Boil notices ran in June 2013 and February 2026, and a pump station project on SW 25th Street runs from October 2025 to October 2026. Details are on the Pembroke Park page.

West Park: Houses From the 1950s and 1960s

West Park incorporated on March 1, 2005 and had 15,130 residents in 2020. About 87 percent of its 4,944 housing units are detached houses, and the median home was built in 1961. Roughly 44 percent went up in the 1950s.

Houses that old often still have their original drain lines and shutoff valves, so we confirm with a camera before quoting. Permits are handled for the city by CAP Government through Broward ePermits. Read more on the West Park page.

Landlords, Tenants and Property Managers

Many calls in Pembroke Park and Dania Beach come from renters, and the person who calls is not always the person who pays. We document what failed with photos and meter readings so owner and tenant can sort out the bill between them.

If you manage several units, tell the dispatcher which building and which unit shares the line. A clog in one unit can be a stack problem that reaches the others.

How Coverage Works

Emergency calls go to one 24/7 line, and the dispatcher routes a plumber based on the address. Distance and traffic change the arrival estimate, so you hear a time when we pick up and not a promise.

ZIP codes do not always follow city lines. Mail addressed to Hollywood can sit inside West Park, and Davie shares ZIPs with its neighbors. Give the street address when you call. Our services page lists everything we repair.

If you are not sure which town you live in, say so on the call. Dania Beach, Davie, Hollywood and Pembroke Park share borders, and the dispatcher can check a street address faster than you can read a map.
