About Dania 24/7 Plumbing

Dania 24/7 Plumbing is an emergency plumbing service based in Dania Beach, FL. This page says plainly how we work and what we leave out.

Who We Are

We are based at 236 N Federal Hwy in Dania Beach and answer the phone around the clock. We send plumbers to Dania Beach, Davie, Pembroke Park and West Park for drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks and pipes.

The site is call-only on purpose. When water is on the floor, a phone call gets you a person and a next step faster than a form.

How We Write About Plumbing

Our guides cite published sources such as the Census Bureau, the City of Dania Beach water report, the Florida Building Code and national cost guides. Where a source does not exist, we say so. For example, no public report gives a hardness number for Dania Beach water, so we do not print one.

Prices on this site are ranges from third-party guides and are not quotes. A real number needs a look at the job.

Check Any Florida Plumber Before You Hire

Florida Statutes chapter 489 requires plumbing contractors to be licensed, and an unlicensed contractor generally cannot enforce a contract. You can look up any contractor by name or license number on the state's MyFloridaLicense site before work starts. The work itself follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Ask for the license number on the phone, confirm the name matches the truck and the paperwork, and ask whether the job needs a permit. Our plumbing installation page explains permits in more detail.

What We Work On

Our work falls into four groups. Drains and sewer lines cover clogs, camera inspections and repairs. Water heaters cover repair, replacement and tankless units. Leaks and supply pipes cover hidden leaks, slab leaks, burst pipes and repiping. Fixtures cover toilets, faucets and disposals, along with new installs and commercial calls.

Every one of these has its own page on the services page, with symptoms, the usual fix and published cost ranges. We would rather you read the page before you call so the conversation starts with the right questions.

Emergency Calls and Scheduled Work

Some jobs cannot wait, such as a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a water heater leaking onto a floor. Others, such as a repipe, a tankless upgrade or a remodel rough-in, can be planned. We take both, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly which one your call is.

For planned work, ask about permits first. Water heater replacement is permitted work in Broward County, and a permit protects you when you sell the house or file an insurance claim.

Who We Serve

Homeowners call us about older houses with original pipe. Renters and landlords call about shared lines and who must pay. Property managers and small businesses call about floor drains, restrooms and after-hours leaks.

We serve Dania Beach, Davie, Pembroke Park and West Park. Each town page lists ZIP codes and local housing notes, and the service areas page shows the full map.

What We Will Not Promise

We will not promise an arrival time before we know the problem and the traffic. We will not quote a firm price by phone for work we have not seen. We will not claim credentials, ratings or years in business that this page cannot back up.

What we do promise is a straight answer about what a phone call cannot tell us, and a plan once someone can see the pipe.

Why the Site Is Call-Only

A form puts your problem in a queue. A phone call puts it in front of a dispatcher who can ask where the main valve is and whether the meter is still moving. Those two questions often decide what happens next.

For that reason every button on this site is a phone link, and there are no forms to fill in. If you would rather write, the email address on the contact page works for questions that are not urgent.

How We Talk About Price

Plumbing prices depend on access, pipe condition, parts and the hour of the call. Because of that, this site shows published ranges from national guides and names each source, so you can judge them yourself.

When you call, we will tell you what usually drives the number for your problem and what we need to see before giving a firm figure. If a quote seems far from the ranges on our pages, ask the plumber to explain the difference. A good answer is specific.
