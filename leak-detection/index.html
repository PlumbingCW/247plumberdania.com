Emergency Leak Detection in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Leak detection in Dania, FL means finding the exact spot where water is escaping before anyone opens a wall, floor or yard. Dania 24/7 Plumbing uses pressure tests, electronic listening and moisture checks to trace hidden leaks across Dania Beach.

Active Leak Right Now? Stop the Water First

If water is spraying, pooling or coming through a ceiling, call us now. Our dispatch line runs 24/7, nights and weekends included. While you wait, close the main shutoff, because a hidden leak does most of its damage while nobody is watching.

Stay out from under a ceiling that is stained, sagging or dripping. Soaked drywall can drop without warning. If a ceiling bulges with water and no light fixture or wiring sits at that spot, a small hole at the lowest point lets it drain into a bucket instead of letting the whole panel fall.

Turn off the breaker for a wet room only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water. Photograph every stain and the meter reading first. Those photos help us trace the water and help your insurer later.

My Water Bill Jumped and I Don't Know Why

EPA WaterSense reports that household leaks waste nearly 1 trillion gallons a year across the country. The average home loses more than 9,300 gallons a year, and about 9 percent of homes leak 50 gallons or more every day. A bill that climbs with no change in habits is often the first sign.

The EPA also notes that a family of four using more than 12,000 gallons a month in winter may have serious leaks. Compare your bill against months when the sprinklers were off. The table shows where most of the rest comes from.

The Meter Keeps Running With the Water Off

This is the cheapest test in plumbing, and the EPA publishes it. Shut off every tap, stop the dishwasher and washing machine, and ask everyone to hold off flushing. Read the meter, wait two hours with no water use, then read it again. Any change means a probable leak. If the dial keeps moving even with the main closed, the leak is probably on the water service line.

The City of Dania Beach runs its own water system, so utility customer service can also tell you what the meter has recorded. We cannot tell you by phone whether a high reading is a leak or a meter quirk. This test separates the two.

  1. Turn off all water use inside and outside, including ice makers and any irrigation timer set to run.
  2. Find the meter box near the curb or property line. Look inside for ants or spiders before you reach in, then lift the lid with a screwdriver.
  3. Write down the full reading, plus the position of the small leak dial or triangle if your meter has one.
  4. If you have a softener or whole-house filter, bypass it first, since a unit that is recharging can move the dial.
  5. Wait two hours without using any water, then read the meter again.
  6. If the reading moved, close the home's main valve and check once more. A dial that stops points to a leak inside the house. A dial that keeps moving points to the line between the meter and the house.

How We Find the Leak Before We Open Anything

We start with questions. When did the bill rise, which fixtures got slow, and where is it damp? Then we isolate. With the main closed, a pressure-test gauge on a hose bib shows whether the system holds. A needle that falls tells us pressurized water is escaping somewhere, and valve-by-valve isolation splits the house into zones: hot side, cold side, irrigation and the line outside.

Next comes electronic listening. A ground microphone and a handheld acoustic sensor pick up the hiss of water leaving a pressurized pipe. An infrared camera shows temperature differences on a wall, and a moisture meter confirms wet drywall. We cross-check two methods before marking a spot.

No method is perfect. Thick concrete and traffic noise dull listening, and a thermal image can show a wet patch that traveled from somewhere else. When the evidence is thin, we say so and test again instead of cutting on a guess.

Water Stain on the Ceiling or a Damp Wall

Water travels. A brown ring on a ceiling often sits under a bathroom, but the leaking pipe can be several feet away, because water follows a joist or a pipe run before it drips. Homes with the air handler in the attic add another suspect. A clogged condensate drain line makes the same stain as a plumbing leak, so we check it early.

Only about 21.7 percent of Dania Beach housing units are detached single-family homes, according to Census data. Many stains therefore come from the unit above. Tell your association or landlord, and ask us to document the source with photos and moisture readings.

Leaks Under the House or the Mobile Home Floor

A house on a slab has nothing under the floor to inspect, but some houses, raised buildings and mobile homes have an open or skirted space beneath. A leak there can run for weeks before anyone sees it. The signs are a floor that feels soft or springy, a musty smell, a damp skirting panel or a meter that keeps moving with every fixture off.

In that space we look at supply lines, drain connections and the water heater drain. We also pressure-test the supply to learn which line holds. Do not crawl into a wet, dark space near wiring. Turn off the main, photograph the access panel and call. We cannot tell by phone whether the water is from a pipe, rain or ground water.

Buried Line Leaks Between the Meter and the House

A buried leak rarely announces itself. Water can drain into porous ground, so the signs are indirect: a meter that runs with every fixture closed, low pressure, a high bill and a patch of lawn that stays green or soft. Our water service line repair page explains who handles each part of that line.

To confirm it, close every fixture and the main valve at the house, then watch the meter. If it still moves, the leak sits between the meter and the house. We trace the run before anyone digs, and Florida law requires an 811 notice before excavation.

Sprinkler, Pool and Yard Leaks

Irrigation runs on its own branch with a backflow preventer, and a break there can waste water for weeks. Signs include a soggy patch in a dry week and one zone with weak heads. The City of Dania Beach operates a Backflow and Cross-Connection Control Program, so ask the city what it requires for your device. We cannot quote a test interval from here.

Pool-adjacent leaks come in two kinds. Leaks in the pool shell or return lines under water belong to a pool company. Leaks in the house supply to the pool, the autofill line or the equipment pad are ours. A bucket test helps sort them out: set a bucket of water on a pool step with the levels matched and compare the drop after a day. If the pool falls faster than the bucket, evaporation is not the whole story.

Commercial Leak Detection for Plazas, Shops and Multi-Unit Buildings

Commercial work is mostly isolation. A multi-tenant plaza along Federal Highway shares risers, and a leak in one suite can show up as a stain in the next. We close valves suite by suite, test each branch and record the path so owners and tenants can settle who pays.

Larger centers such as Dania Pointe carry backflow assemblies and fire-service lines. We handle domestic water, and fire lines go to a fire-protection contractor. For shop and office plumbing beyond leak tracing, see our commercial plumbing page.

What to Check First Before You Call

These checks take ten minutes. Skip anything that means climbing into a hot attic, and leave electrical panels alone.

  • Toilet dye test: put food coloring in the tank and wait 10 minutes. Color in the bowl means a leaking flapper.
  • Look under each sink and behind each toilet for a damp cabinet floor, soft wood or rust on the shutoff valve.
  • Check the water heater base and pan for moisture, and look at the T&P discharge pipe for drips.
  • Run the irrigation zones one at a time and watch for geysers or soggy ground.
  • Listen at night with the house quiet. A faint hiss near a wall or the water heater is worth a call.
  • Photograph the meter before and after your two-hour test.

What Leak Detection Costs in South Florida

National published guides put slab leak detection at about $150 to $400, with one guide reaching near $600. A pipe leak diagnostic fee runs about $80 to $100 in another guide. Florida prices vary, and we have not found Broward-specific figures to quote.

Detection is separate from repair. Finding the leak is one job, and fixing the pipe is a second job priced by what is broken and how deep it sits.

What moves the price

  • Access. A leak behind a tub wall takes more time than one under an open crawl area.
  • Methods needed. A simple pressure test costs less than a full search with listening gear and thermal imaging.
  • Pipe location. Under a slab or in a finished ceiling raises the effort. A line in the yard is easier to reach.
  • Size of the property. A multi-unit building with several risers needs more isolation steps than a single house.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle leak detection emergencies at night or on weekends in Dania?

Yes. Our dispatch line runs 24/7. We cannot promise an arrival time over the phone, but we will tell you what to shut off and what to photograph while a plumber is on the way.

How do I know if the leak is under my slab or inside a wall?

A warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water with every fixture off, and a meter that keeps moving all point toward the slab. A stain on a wall or ceiling usually points to a wall or attic line. Only testing confirms it, and our slab leak repair page explains the next steps.

My water bill jumped and I don't know why. What should I do first?

Run the two-hour meter test described above and do the toilet dye test. If the meter moves with everything off, call us. If it holds still, look at irrigation timers, a pool autofill or a change in how many people live in the house.

Is the leak in my pool or in my pipes?

Use the bucket test first. If the pool drops faster than the bucket, the pool shell or its return lines may be leaking, and a pool company handles those. If the pool level is fine but your meter runs, the house supply or autofill line is the suspect, and we can test it.

I manage rental property in Dania. Can you document a leak for owners and tenants?

Yes. We note the location, the method that found it, moisture readings and photos, then give you a written summary. We also serve Davie and the rest of South Broward, so one visit can cover more than one building.

How long does a leak search take?

A simple search can take an hour or two, and a hidden slab leak can take longer. We cannot give a fixed time before we see the house. Call (954) 697-4920 and we will walk you through what to expect.

Another plumber could not find my leak. Can you?

We cannot promise to find what another plumber missed, but we start from what was already ruled out. Tell us what was tested, and we use the meter test and pressure checks to narrow the run before anything is opened.
