Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Sewer line repair fixes the pipe itself when a crack, root-split joint, offset or collapse keeps wastewater from leaving a Dania Beach home. It ranges from a short spot repair to trenchless lining to full replacement, and a camera decides which one fits.

Call (954) 697-4920

Sewage in the Yard or Under the Floor? Emergency Steps

A broken sewer line is a health hazard, and our dispatch runs 24/7 so you do not wait for morning. Stop all water use in the house, including washing machines and showers. Every gallon you send down a broken line leaves through the break and into the soil or under the slab.

Keep children and pets away from wet ground and any pooled water, and do not wade through it. Stay off wet floors near outlets. Tell us on the call where the smell or the wet spot is, because that is our first clue to where the pipe has failed.

Signs the Sewer Line Is Broken, Not Just Blocked

Callers describe it as water coming up under the floor, a wet patch in the yard that never dries, or a sewer smell that no drain cleaner touches. Other clues are a sinkhole forming over the lateral, toilets that clear then back up again within days, and a cable that keeps stopping at one distance.

A blockage can be cleared with sewer line cleaning or a hydro jetter. A break cannot, because the water that escapes through the crack carries soil back into the pipe. If your line has been cleared more than once for the same symptom, a sewer camera inspection is the honest next step.

Trenchless Pipe Lining and Pipe Bursting, Explained

Pipe lining, called cured-in-place pipe, pulls a resin-soaked tube into the old pipe, inflates it and lets it harden into a new pipe inside the old one. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through while splitting the old one. Both work from one or two small access pits, so the yard stays mostly intact.

Trenchless is not always possible. A pipe that has collapsed or shifted out of line may not accept a liner, and a bellied section stays bellied. We tell you when trenchless fits and when it does not, with the camera footage as proof.

Open-Trench Repair, Sunshine 811 and Groundwater

When digging is the right answer, the first call is to Sunshine 811. Florida law, Statute 556.105, requires notice at least two full business days before digging, weekends and holidays excluded, and the marked tolerance zone is 24 inches from each facility's outside edge. Peoples Gas serves parts of Broward, so a gas line may cross your lateral.

USGS describes the top of the Biscayne aquifer as at or near the land surface across most of its area, so a deep trench in Dania Beach can fill with groundwater and needs pumping. That is one reason lining is attractive here when the pipe allows it.

What to Do Before We Arrive

These steps limit damage and give us a clear start.

  • Stop using all fixtures and the washing machine.
  • Mark the wet or sunken spot with a flag or a stick, and take a photo so we can see how it changes.
  • Do not dig it up yourself. A shovel can hit a gas, electric or water line, and Sunshine 811 notice is required first.
  • Move vehicles and heavy items off the area, since soil over a leaking pipe can be soft.
  • Gather any plan, permit or past plumber report that shows where the line runs.

Sewer Line Repair Cost Ranges

National published guides put spot repair of a cracked sewer pipe at about $600 to $1,050 (Angi, 2026). Full replacement runs about $50 to $250 per linear foot, and Florida prices vary a great deal by access and depth.

What moves the price

  • Length of pipe. Cost scales with feet. A 6-foot spot repair is not priced like a 60-foot replacement.
  • Depth. Deeper pipe means more digging, shoring and groundwater control.
  • Access. A pipe under a driveway, patio or slab raises the cost of any open-trench option.
  • Method. Lining, bursting and trenching each carry different equipment and restoration costs.
  • Restoration. Concrete, pavers, sod and landscaping after the work can cost as much as the pipe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle sewer line repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Dania?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, and sewage in a yard or under a floor is treated as an emergency. We cannot promise an arrival time, but we tell you how to limit water use while you wait.

Can you fix just the cracked section where roots got in, or does the whole line have to be replaced?

Often a spot repair is enough, but only if the camera shows the rest of the pipe is sound. Angi lists spot repair of a cracked sewer pipe at about $600 to $1,050. If the pipe is cracked in several places or deformed, patches keep chasing the next failure and replacement or lining makes more sense.

Will you dig up my whole yard?

Not if the pipe allows lining or bursting, which need only small pits. If the pipe has collapsed or shifted, a trench may be the only repair. We show you the camera footage that decides it.

Do I need a permit for sewer line repair in Dania Beach?

Permit rules vary, so ask the Dania Beach Building Division what your address needs. We cannot confirm local rules by phone.

Who is responsible for the sewer line, me or the city?

We cannot tell you by phone. The ownership line between your lateral and the city main varies, so ask the Dania Beach sewer office. Our footage shows where the break sits so you can point to the exact spot.

I own a rental. Who should call?

Either the owner or the property manager can call. Tell us which units are affected, and we will document the line with a camera so the repair decision is clear.
