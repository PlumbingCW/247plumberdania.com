Water Softener Service in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

A water softener is a planned plumbing job, and the first question is whether your water needs one at all. Dania 24/7 Plumbing helps Dania Beach homeowners test the tap, read the utility's water report and, if a softener makes sense, plumb it in correctly.

Does Dania Beach Water Need a Softener?

Start with the facts your utility publishes. The City of Dania Beach 2024 water quality report says its plant uses lime softening and nanofiltration and blends the two streams. That report does not print a hardness number, so we will not guess one for you.

Hardness varies by street and by utility, and a lime-softened supply can still leave scale in a hot water heater. The reliable answer comes from your own tap, which is why testing comes before any equipment is chosen.

What Hard Water Does Inside a House

The U.S. Geological Survey says calcium reacts with soap to form scum, so you need more soap for hands, hair and laundry. It also says heating hard water leaves calcium carbonate scale that can shorten equipment life, raise heating costs and clog pipes.

Scale shows up first where water is heated or sprayed. Look for white crust on showerheads and faucet aerators, spots on glassware and a tank water heater that rumbles. Our water heater repair page covers the tank side, and the tankless water heater page covers flush and descale work.

  • White crust around faucet aerators and showerheads
  • Spots or film on glassware after the dishwasher runs
  • A tank heater that rumbles or pops as it heats
  • Soap that will not lather the way it should

Test Your Tap Before You Buy Anything

The USGS advises asking the local health agency, the water utility or the vendor of a local softening system about hardness at your address. We add one step first: test the water that actually comes out of your faucet.

  1. Ask your water utility for its latest annual water quality report and look for hardness.
  2. Buy a hardness test strip or send a sample to a lab, and test a cold tap before any softener.
  3. Compare the result with the USGS classes: soft is 0 to 60 mg/L, moderately hard 61 to 120, hard 121 to 180 and very hard above 180.
  4. Note any scale you can see on fixtures and the water heater.
  5. Call us with the numbers if you decide to add or service equipment.

The Plumbing Side of a Softener Install

A softener ties into the main water line after the meter and the main shutoff. It needs a bypass so you can shut it off without losing water, a drain line for its regeneration water, and a nearby outlet. Where the unit sits matters, because many homes in Dania have tight garages and closets.

Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Dania Beach Building Division whether the job needs one at your address. The same pipe fitting, shutoff and drain work appears in our plumbing installation jobs, and a new heater is covered on the water heater installation page.

A Softener That Stopped Working or Started Leaking

Owners tell us the same few things. The water feels hard again, the unit runs all the time, salt will not move, or water collects around the brine tank. We can check the plumbing around the unit, including the bypass, the supply and drain connections and any leaking fitting.

If the fault sits in the control head or the resin tank, the maker's parts and service may be needed. Angi also lists a failing softener among the causes of a high water bill, so a running unit is worth a look. See leak detection if the meter will not stop.

What a Water Softener Costs in South Florida

National published guides vary a lot. Angi puts water softener installation at about $200 to $6,000, with installation labor of about $150 to $1,000. Florida prices vary, and we have no Broward figure to quote.

The equipment you choose drives the number more than the pipe work does.

What moves the price

  • Type and size. Household size and water use set how big the unit needs to be.
  • Location. A far closet needs more pipe and a longer drain run than a garage by the main.
  • Drain and power. A nearby drain line and outlet keep the install short.
  • Bypass and fittings. Old shutoff valves or damaged pipe at the connection point add work.
  • Permit questions. Ask the Building Division what your address needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dania Beach water hard?

The city's 2024 water quality report says it softens water with lime and nanofiltration but does not print a hardness figure. Ask the city, then test your own tap, because hardness can differ by address.

How do I find my water hardness in Dania?

Ask your utility for its annual water quality report, then test a cold tap with a strip or a lab kit. The USGS suggests asking the utility, the local health agency or a softener vendor.

Does Davie water need a softener?

That depends on which utility serves you. The Town of Davie serves only a portion of Davie, so read your bill to see who supplies your house and ask them for hardness. Our Davie page lists what we cover there.

Will a softener change my plumbing?

It adds a bypass on the main line, a supply and return connection and a drain line for its regeneration water. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Dania Beach Building Division about your address.

My softener stopped softening. Can you look at it?

We can check the plumbing around it, including the bypass, the connections and the drain line. A fault in the control head or the resin tank may need the maker's service. We cannot diagnose it by phone.

Is a leaking softener an emergency?

Close the bypass if you can, and close the home's main valve if water is spreading. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Call (954) 697-4920 and tell us where the water is and whether it is still running.

Code reference: plumbing work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (8th Edition, 2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether your job needs a permit.
