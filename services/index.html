Drains and Sewer Lines

For clogs, backups and damaged lines, from a single sink to the main line.

Drain Cleaning

Drain cleaning clears whatever stops a sink, tub, shower or floor drain from emptying, and it is the call we hear most often in Dania, FL.

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting sends a controlled stream of water through a drain or sewer line at high pressure to scour grease, scale and silt off the pipe wall.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries every fixture's wastewater out of a Dania Beach house toward the street connection.

Sewer Line Repair

Sewer line repair fixes the pipe itself when a crack, root-split joint, offset or collapse keeps wastewater from leaving a Dania Beach home.

Sewer Inspection

A sewer camera inspection runs a waterproof camera through your main sewer line so you can see cracks, roots, offsets and buildup on screen.

Water Heaters

Tank and tankless units, from a failed element to a full replacement.

Water Heater Repair

Water heater repair in Dania Beach means finding out which part stopped working, whether that is a heating element, a gas control valve or a relief valve, and fixing it when the repair is worth the money.

Water Heater Replacement

Water heater replacement in Dania Beach is the answer when a tank has rusted through, a repair costs more than the unit is worth, or the heater is past its working life.

Water Heater Installation

Water heater installation in Dania Beach is the planned version of a swap: you choose the fuel, size and location before the old tank fails.

Tankless Water Heater

A tankless water heater in Dania Beach heats water only when a tap opens, using a gas burner or electric elements instead of a stored tank.

Leaks and Supply Pipes

Hidden water, burst pipes and the lines that feed your house.

Leak Detection

Leak detection in Dania, FL means finding the exact spot where water is escaping before anyone opens a wall, floor or yard.

Slab Leak Repair

A slab leak is a break in a water line that runs in or under the concrete floor of a house.

Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe repair stops water that is pouring from a split or broken line and replaces the failed section.

Water Service Line Repair

The water service line is the buried pipe that carries city water from the meter to your house.

Repiping

Repiping replaces the water supply pipes inside a house, from the main valve to every faucet, with new PEX-A or copper.

Water Softener Service

A water softener is a planned plumbing job, and the first question is whether your water needs one at all.

Fixtures and Commercial

Toilets, faucets, disposals, new installs and after-hours business calls.

Toilet Repair

Toilet repair in Dania, FL means fixing whatever keeps a toilet from flushing, filling or sealing to the floor, from a worn flapper to a cracked closet flange.

Faucet Repair

Faucet repair in Dania, FL covers the handle, cartridge, seat, aerator and supply stop that sit between the pipe in your wall and the water in your sink.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Garbage disposal repair in Dania, FL covers jams, leaks, dead motors and the dishwasher drain connection on the unit under your kitchen sink.

Plumbing Installation

Plumbing installation in Dania, FL is the planned side of the trade: new fixtures, relocated supply and drain lines, and the rough-in behind a remodeled bathroom or kitchen.

Commercial Plumbing
