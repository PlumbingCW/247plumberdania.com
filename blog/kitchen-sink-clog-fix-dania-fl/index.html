Kitchen Sink Clog Fix in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Most kitchen sink clogs in Dania, FL sit in the disposal, the P-trap or the first few feet of branch pipe, and you can clear many of them with a plunger and a bucket. Skip the chemical openers. If the water backs up elsewhere in the house, the clog is deeper and needs a plumber.

Find the real clog: one bowl, two bowls or the disposal

Start by noticing where the water goes. If a double sink drains one side and fills the other, the clog is past the shared tailpiece. If both bowls hold water and the disposal hums or does nothing, the disposal may be jammed. If the dishwasher drain hose joins the disposal, a clog there can also push dishwasher water into the sink.

Run the cold tap for ten seconds in the bathroom sink. If that drain is slow too, the clog is in a shared line and not just under the kitchen counter.

Safe steps to try first

These steps use common tools and need no special skill. Stop if anything feels wrong, and do not mix products.

  1. Turn off the disposal switch and unplug the unit or switch off its breaker. Never put a hand in a disposal.
  2. Pour a pot of hot tap water, not boiling water, down the drain. Boiling water can soften PVC joints.
  3. Plunge with a flat cup plunger. Plug the other bowl with a wet rag, cover the drain fully and push and pull for 20 seconds.
  4. If the disposal is jammed, insert a hex key into the center hole underneath and turn it back and forth, then press the red reset button.
  5. Set a bucket under the trap, loosen the two slip nuts by hand, pull the U shaped P-trap and clear it into the bucket. Rinse it and reassemble hand tight.
  6. Run water for a minute and check every joint for drips.

What Dania Beach Homeowners Should Know About Kitchen Drains

Kitchen sink water goes into the sanitary sewer, and Dania Beach wastewater is sent to the City of Hollywood's Southern Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant. Storm drains are a separate system. The City of Dania Beach describes them as designed exclusively for stormwater runoff, so grease, oil and rinse water should never go there.

The Census ACS counts 16,348 housing units in Dania Beach, and about 57 percent of them sit in buildings with two or more units. Only 21.7 percent are detached single family homes. In a duplex, condo or apartment building, kitchens often share a vertical drain stack, so one tenant's grease can back up the unit below. Report a repeat clog to the landlord or association, and ask for the stack to be cleaned.

We cannot tell you from public pages who owns each section of pipe in your building, so check your lease or association documents.

Why grease is the usual villain

Hot grease runs freely down the drain and then cools in the pipe. It coats the walls, collects coffee grounds and food bits, and slowly narrows the line. Hot water does not carry it far, because the grease hardens again a few feet downstream.

Wipe pans with a paper towel and throw it away. Keep a jar for cooking oil, and let it cool before it goes in the trash. A drain cable can open the pipe once it narrows, but a coating that long may need hydro jetting to remove.

Garbage disposal habits that prevent clogs

A disposal grinds food into small pieces and relies on water to wash them away. It does not make everything safe to send down. Fibrous and starchy foods are the common culprits.

If it hums but does not spin, a hex key often frees it. If it leaks, grinds loudly or trips the reset repeatedly, it needs service from a garbage disposal repair visit.

  • Run cold water before, during and for 15 seconds after grinding.
  • Skip celery strings, potato peels, pasta and rice, which swell and pack.
  • Skip coffee grounds, eggshell fragments and bones.
  • Never pour grease, oil or fat in, even with hot water.
  • Cut large scraps into small pieces and feed them slowly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner in a kitchen sink?

We advise against it. The chemicals can sit in the pipe, damage the disposal seals and burn skin when you remove the trap. If the product does not work, the plumber has to deal with the chemical too. A plunger and the P-trap are safer.

Why does my kitchen sink clog every few months?

A greasy coating builds up on the pipe wall and narrows it, so each small scrap catches. A cable clears a channel but leaves the coating behind. Repeated clogs call for a camera look and probably a jetting pass.

Why does my condo kitchen sink back up when the unit above runs water?

Many Dania Beach buildings share a vertical drain stack between units. A blockage below the neighbor's connection pushes water back into the lowest kitchen. Tell your property manager, because the stack is usually a building repair.

Can I put coffee grounds or rice down the disposal?

No. Grounds pack into a sludge, and rice swells with water. Put both in the trash or a compost bin.

Should I call at night for a kitchen sink clog?

If water is overflowing or sewage is coming up, yes. A slow sink with no standing water can wait for morning. Call (954) 697-4920 and we will help you judge.
