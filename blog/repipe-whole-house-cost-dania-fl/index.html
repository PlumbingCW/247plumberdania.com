Whole House Repipe Cost in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

National guides put a whole-house repipe at roughly $1,500 to $15,000, and Florida prices vary with house size and pipe material. In Dania, FL a repipe makes sense after repeated leaks, polybutylene or galvanized pipe, not after a single failure.

Repipe or keep patching? A decision table

One leak does not condemn a whole system. Angi's repipe guide lists the real triggers: pipes past 50 to 60 years old, galvanized or lead pipe, polybutylene, leaks that keep returning after patches, and corrosion or discolored water. The page gives no hard threshold, so the call is a judgment between you and a plumber.

This table turns that list into a first read. It is a guide, not a diagnosis, and a plumber has to look at the pipe itself.

My pipes keep leaking: what that usually means

Repeated leaks in separate spots tell a plumber more than one big failure does. When the pipe wall is thin everywhere, patching one spot just moves the pressure to the next weak one. Each patch also costs a service call and a wet-floor repair.

Before pricing a repipe, ask for a pressure test and a look at the pipe's inside surface. Our leak detection team uses acoustic listening and thermal tools to find hidden leaks without opening walls first. That tells you whether you have one bad run or a failing system.

What Dania Beach Homeowners Should Know About Pipe Age

Census ACS data puts the median Dania Beach home at a 1977 build year, so half the housing is older than that. The 1970s account for 31.2 percent of units, the 1950s for 14.2 percent, and the 1980s for 14.6 percent. Those decades predate today's common plastic supply pipe, so check what your lines are made of.

The polybutylene window of about 1978 to 1995 overlaps the 1980s stock, so a house from that decade deserves a quick look at its supply lines. Homes built before 1978 may still have been repiped in that window, which is another reason to inspect rather than assume.

Dania Beach also has many multi-unit buildings. Only 21.7 percent of its 16,348 housing units are detached single-family homes. In a condo, the repipe often runs through shared walls, so the association and the neighboring unit matter. We also repipe homes in Pembroke Park and Davie.

The polybutylene years and what they mean today

Polybutylene, or PB, is a gray plastic supply pipe. WRAL reported that it was used in homes from about 1978 to 1995. A 1995 class action settlement let qualifying owners claim part of the replumbing cost, but that fund has long since closed in practice, so do not count on it.

If you find gray plastic pipe with plastic or acetal fittings, treat it as a repipe candidate and tell your insurer's agent honestly. We could not confirm any federal agency guidance on PB, so rely on a plumber's inspection and your own policy.

What a whole-house repipe costs, with sources

Published ranges are wide because house size, pipe choice and wall access change the work. Angi gives a typical $1,500 to $15,000, with most homeowners paying around $7,500. Forbes lists $3,000 to $16,000 for a 2,000 square foot home.

Per foot, Angi shows PEX at about $0.40 to $2 and copper at $2 to $8. Forbes runs higher, with PEX at $1.50 to $4 and copper at $4 to $12, labor included. None of these are Florida figures, and Broward prices vary, so a written estimate from a licensed plumber is the only real number.

PEX or copper for a South Florida house

PEX is flexible plastic tubing. It threads through walls with fewer joints and usually less wall patching. PEX-A uses expansion rings, and PEX-B uses crimp rings. Copper is rigid and has a long record, but it costs more and needs soldered joints.

Copper has local baggage. A class action filed against Miramar alleges the city's treated water reacts with copper and causes pinhole leaks. The city says its water meets standards and blames installation or grounding, so these are claims, not findings. If you choose copper, ask your plumber about proper grounding and fittings.

What a repipe job involves

A repipe replaces the supply lines from the main shutoff to each fixture. The drain and sewer lines are separate and stay unless they also fail. Plan for the water to be off during work and for small access holes in drywall that get patched after.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether a repipe at your address needs one and who pulls it. Settle that before work begins.

  • Ask for the pipe material, brand and warranty in writing
  • Confirm the contractor's licence on the DBPR Licensee Search
  • Ask how many days the water will be off and how they patch drywall
  • Ask whether the quote includes the permit and the final inspection
  • Photograph every room before work starts

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a whole house repipe cost in Dania, FL?

National guides put it at about $1,500 to $15,000 (Angi) or $3,000 to $16,000 for a 2,000 square foot home (Forbes). Florida prices vary. We cannot quote a number by phone without seeing the house.

Does a 1977 house in Dania Beach need a repipe?

Not automatically. The median home was built in 1977, and many have been repaired or repiped since. Age plus repeated leaks, discolored water or a known bad pipe type is what triggers a repipe.

How do I know if I have polybutylene pipe?

It is usually gray, and it was used from about 1978 to 1995 according to WRAL. Check the water heater connections, under sinks and near the main shutoff. A plumber can identify it on sight.

Is repiping covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not as maintenance. Policies differ, and some cover a sudden leak but not the pipe's replacement. Read your policy or ask your agent before the work.

Can a repipe be done in a Dania Beach condo?

It can, but the association may restrict the schedule and require approvals. Shared walls make it complex. Call Call (954) 697-4920 and we will tell you what to ask your board.

Code reference: plumbing work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (8th Edition, 2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask the Dania Beach Building Division whether your job needs a permit.
