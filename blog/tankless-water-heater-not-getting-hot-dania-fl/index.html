Tankless Water Heater Not Getting Hot? What to Check in Dania, FL

By the Dania 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

If your tankless water heater is not getting hot, check four things first: power, the gas valve, the display for an error code, and whether hot water is too low in flow to trigger the unit. Anything involving gas parts, venting or the burner is a job for a plumber in Dania, FL.

The hot water went cold or lukewarm. Where do I start?

Start by noticing the pattern, because it points to the cause. A unit that gives no heat at all behaves differently from one that gives warm water that fades, or one that works at the kitchen sink but not the shower.

Ask three quick questions. Does it fail at every tap or just one? Does the display show a number or a letter code? Did anything change in the last day, such as a power flicker or a work crew on the street? Write the answers down, because they help whoever diagnoses it.

Checks a homeowner can do safely

These steps need no tools and no opening of panels. Stop the moment anything smells like gas.

  1. Look at the display. Write down any error code and find it in the manual, which usually lists a meaning.
  2. Check the breaker for the unit and for any nearby outlet. Flip a tripped breaker off and on once. If it trips again, stop.
  3. Confirm the gas shutoff on a gas model is in the open position, with the handle in line with the pipe. Do not relight pilots on parts you do not know.
  4. Check that the hot and cold isolation valves at the bottom are fully open.
  5. Run the hot tap at a steady medium flow. Tankless units need a minimum flow to fire, so a barely open tap can stay cold.
  6. Try a second hot tap. If one tap is cold and the other is hot, the problem may be a faucet cartridge, not the heater.

Why a tankless heater runs cold when two taps are open

The heater has a maximum flow it can heat. DOE says demand-type units typically deliver about 2 to 5 gallons per minute. If a shower and a dishwasher both draw hot water, the unit may reach its limit and the temperature drops at both.

Turn one tap off and wait 30 seconds. If the water warms up, the heater is working but undersized for your peak use. That is a sizing problem, not a failure, and the answer may be a larger unit or a second one.

Hard water and scale can stop a tankless heater from firing

Family Handyman notes that mineral buildup can keep a tankless unit from switching on, and that tank heaters need yearly flushing. Scale coats the heat exchanger and narrows small passages, and the unit may throw a code or shut down to protect itself.

Flushing a tankless unit means connecting hoses to service valves and circulating a descaling solution, as your manual directs. Some owners do this themselves. If you have never done it, or the valves are stuck, we would rather you call than force a fitting and cause a leak.

When to stop and call a plumber

Some problems are not safe to chase on your own. Call if any of these appear:

  • You smell gas. Leave the house, call 911 from outside, then call your gas utility.
  • The display shows an ignition, flame or exhaust error that returns after one reset.
  • You see water leaking from the unit or the vent.
  • There is soot, discoloration or a burning smell around the unit.
  • The breaker keeps tripping.
  • Water is only lukewarm even after you have checked flow and valves.

What to have ready before you call us

A good description saves time. Tell us the brand and the rough age from the rating label, whether the unit burns gas or uses electricity, and the exact error code on the display.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a water main break or boil water notice stop my tankless heater from heating in Dania Beach?

It can. Low flow or pressure from the street can keep the flow sensor from firing the unit, and sediment can come loose after work on a main. Check the city's notices, clean the inlet filter if your manual allows it, and call us if flow stays weak.

Is it safe to reset my tankless water heater?

A single reset by breaker or the power button is generally fine for most models. Do not reset repeatedly. If a code returns, there is a cause that needs a plumber.

Why does my tankless heater give hot water for a minute then go cold?

Common causes are too much demand, scale on the heat exchanger or a flame sensor problem. We cannot tell which by phone, but the error code helps.

Should I drain a tankless heater myself?

Descaling is a maintenance task that some owners do with the manufacturer's instructions. Do not open sealed panels, touch gas parts or force stuck valves.
