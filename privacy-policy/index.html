Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This policy explains what information Dania 24/7 Plumbing receives and how we use it.

Call (954) 697-4920

What We Collect

This website has no forms, accounts or checkout. We receive information only when you contact us. If you call, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell the dispatcher, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem. If you email, we receive your email address and the message.

How We Use It

We use what you share to answer your question, send a plumber, give an estimate or follow up on a job. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services on This Site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps. When your browser loads them, Google can receive your IP address and browser details under Google's own privacy policy. We do not control that data.

If we add analytics or advertising tools later, we will update this page first.

Phone Calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We also note what you tell the dispatcher so that a plumber has the details.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you gave us by emailing contact@247plumberdania.com. Our address is 236 N Federal Hwy, Dania Beach, FL 33004. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
