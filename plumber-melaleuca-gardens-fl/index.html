Melaleuca Gardens at a Glance

Houses, not apartments

Listing data describes the neighborhood as single-family homes. In a house, the plumbing on your side of the water meter is yours to maintain, so ask the City's utility office where its responsibility ends at your address.

A 45-year build window

The same data lists homes from 1950 to 1995. A 1951 house and a 1993 house on neighboring streets can hide very different supply lines, drains and shutoff valves.

Pools and some docks

Listing descriptions mention private pools and, for some homes, private docks. A pool fill line, an outdoor spigot or a dock water line each adds a place for water to escape outside the house.

Galvanized Steel and the Slow Loss of Pressure

Houses from the early 1950s may still carry galvanized steel supply pipe. The pipe rusts from the inside, the opening narrows and pressure falls long before anything leaks. Brown water after the house sits idle, a weak shower and pinholes at threaded joints are the usual signs.

Open a bathroom tap and the kitchen tap together. If the flow collapses at both, tell us when you call. A pressure gauge on a hose bib and a look at the pipe near the meter separate a partly closed valve from a corroded line, and a repiping quote only makes sense after that.

Polybutylene: Checking the 1978 to 1995 Question

Polybutylene is a gray, blue or black plastic supply pipe that went into many American homes from about 1978 to 1995. Some houses in this build window may have it, and some never did. Look at the pipe near your water heater and where the line enters the house, and note any marking that starts with the letters PB.

If you find it, do not panic and do not cut into it yourself. Call (954) 697-4920 and we will inspect the connections, check the fittings at the heater and tell you what the findings mean for water heater replacement or a larger repipe.

Drains Under the Slab and Roots in the Yard

A house built in the 1950s may drain through cast iron or clay pipe buried under the slab and in the yard. Roots find the joints, and cast iron scales and thins over decades. When two or more fixtures slow down together, the problem is usually the main line and not a single trap.

Start with sewer line cleaning to clear the blockage, then a sewer inspection camera to see why it formed. If the camera shows a collapsed section, sewer line repair is the next conversation, and we show you the footage first. The City describes Dania Beach as flat and low, so a heavy rain can slow a drain that is already tight.

Pools, Docks and Outdoor Water Lines

Outdoor plumbing leaks quietly. A pool auto-fill that sticks open, a hose bib that drips inside the wall or an old dock supply line can run for weeks before the water bill shows it. Close every tap, then watch the meter's small leak dial. If it keeps turning, water is escaping somewhere.

That single test tells us whether to start with leak detection or with a fixture. If the loss is between the meter and the house, water service line repair is the likely fix. A dripping outdoor spigot is a quick faucet repair and not an emergency.

Scale, Fixtures and the City's Water Report

The City of Dania Beach publishes an annual water quality report. The 2024 edition describes lime softening and nanofiltration and does not give a hardness figure, so we do not quote one for your tap. Spots on glass, crust around aerators and a heater that rumbles are the practical signs of mineral buildup.

A flushed tank heater and clean aerators solve many of those complaints. If they return, a water softener or a heater replacement may be worth pricing. Ask us which fits a house of your age.

Permits, Code and Who to Ask

Plumbing in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, 8th Edition (2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code. Whether your job needs a permit depends on the work, so ask the Dania Beach Building Division before a repipe or a water heater swap.

The City lists Melaleuca Gardens as OASIS 8, a neighborhood project that covers street sign and stop sign replacement. City crews on your street are not the water utility, and a cone on the corner does not explain low pressure inside your house.

Before We Arrive: Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Melaleuca Gardens

Close the main valve

Turn the shutoff near the meter or where the line enters the house. Older valves can stick, so turn slowly and tell us if it will not move.

Stop the heater

Switch off the breaker to an electric water heater. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility first.

Protect the floor

Lift rugs, towels and anything on the floor. Brick and block houses on slabs can hold water against baseboards for days.

Frequently Asked Questions: Melaleuca Gardens

Do you cover all of Melaleuca Gardens?

Yes. Listings place the neighborhood in ZIP 33004 along NW 6th to NW 10th Avenues and NW 7th and 8th Streets. Give us your cross street when you call so dispatch finds you quickly.

My house was built in the 1950s. Do I need to repipe?

Not automatically. We test pressure, look at the pipe near the meter and heater, and run a camera down the drain if needed. A repipe is a larger project, and the findings decide it, not the age alone.

How do I know if I have polybutylene pipe?

Look for gray, blue or black flexible plastic near the water heater or where the line enters the house, often with markings that start with PB. Send us the street address and we will check the connections on site.

My water bill jumped and I have a pool. Where do we start?

Close every tap and watch the meter leak dial. If it moves, the loss is somewhere on the property. Pool fill lines, spigots and the line from the meter are the first places we trace.

Can I call about a plumbing emergency in Melaleuca Gardens?

Call (954) 697-4920 and describe the problem. Shut the water at the main valve first if a pipe is leaking, and ask on the call what arrival time is realistic for your street.

City crews are working on my street. Does that explain my low pressure?

Not by itself. The City lists the Melaleuca Gardens project as street sign and stop sign replacement. If pressure dropped inside the house, call us and we will test it.
